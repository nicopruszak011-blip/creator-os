# Privacy Policy – ItsNax0 Creator OS

Last updated: November 25, 2026

## 1. Who we are

ItsNax0 Creator OS ("the App") is a private desktop tool used by the content creator ItsNax0 to cut clips from his own Twitch streams and publish them to his own clip accounts on TikTok and YouTube. The App is operated by Nicolas Pruszak, c/o POSTFLEX PFX-529-851, Emsdettener Straße 10, 48268 Greven, Germany. Contact: business@itsnaxo.de

## 2. Who uses the App

The App is used only by ItsNax0 and only with accounts he owns, for example itsNax0.AE, ItsNax0.moments, ItsNax0.Clips and the YouTube channel ItsNax0 Reaction. It is not offered to the public and does not process data of other users.

## 3. Data we process

TikTok account data received via TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API: open ID, display name, username, avatar URL, the privacy options and interaction settings returned by the creator info endpoint, and the publish ID and status of uploaded videos.

Access tokens for TikTok and YouTube that are needed to upload videos on the creator's behalf.

Video files and captions created by the App from the creator's own stream recordings.

Public statistics of the creator's own uploaded videos, such as views and likes.

We do not collect data about viewers, followers or any other TikTok users.

## 4. How we use the data

To upload videos to the creator's own accounts only after the creator has reviewed them and chosen the visibility, interaction settings and commercial content disclosure in the App. To show the creator which account he is posting to. To display the status and performance of his own uploads.

## 5. Storage and security

All data is stored locally on the creator's own computer. Access tokens are stored encrypted and are never shared. The App has no cloud database and no third-party analytics.

## 6. Sharing

Data is only sent to the official APIs of TikTok (Content Posting API, Login Kit) and Google/YouTube (YouTube Data API) for the purposes above. We do not sell or share data with anyone else.

## 7. Retention and deletion

Tokens are deleted when the creator disconnects an account in the App or revokes access in his TikTok settings under Security and permissions, Apps. Local video files and statistics can be deleted at any time. On request to business@itsnaxo.de all stored data is deleted.

## 8. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectify, delete and restrict processing of your data and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Contact: business@itsnaxo.de

## 9. Changes

We will update this page if the App changes how it handles data.
