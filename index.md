# ItsNax0 Creator OS

ItsNax0 Creator OS is a private desktop tool of the streamer ItsNax0. It cuts short clips from his own Twitch streams and publishes them to his own TikTok and YouTube clip accounts. Before anything is posted, the creator reviews each clip, sees which TikTok account it goes to, edits the caption, picks the privacy level, decides about comments, duets and stitches, and discloses commercial content if needed.
